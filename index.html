I am an architect, and I teach and do research at the School of Architecture of the Universidad de Navarra in Pamplona.

My work sits between building physics and data: energy simulation with EnergyPlus and OpenStudio, multi-objective optimisation, climate data for design, and the performance of concrete and high-performance industrial floors.

On this site you will find my research and publications, and the small browser tools I build for my students.

Browser apps

They run in the browser: nothing to install, and your files never leave your computer.

  • EPW Climate Analyzer

    Reads an EnergyPlus weather file, hourly or every 10 minutes, and draws the design conditions sheet of the IDAE guides, wind roses, a psychrometric chart, degree-days, hour-by-month tables and heat and cold waves. A world map lists about 100,000 free weather files from Climate.OneBuilding.Org and EnergyPlus.net.

Research

Building energy simulation
Detailed models in EnergyPlus and OpenStudio to understand how buildings use energy, and to design them so that they need less.
Optimisation and decision
Multi-objective optimisation with evolutionary algorithms, coupled to simulation, and multi-criteria methods to choose among the resulting designs.
Climate data for design
Weather files, their analysis and their limits: what a typical year hides about extremes, and how local measurements can improve it.
Materials
Concrete technology, high-performance industrial floors and phase change materials for thermal storage.

About

I trained as an architect. I teach at the School of Architecture of the Universidad de Navarra, where I direct the Department of Building Construction, Services and Structures.

I like tools that show how things work, so that the people who use them can understand and defend their results. Most of what I build is in Python and runs on open software.

Contact: gramrui@unav.es. School of Architecture, Universidad de Navarra, Pamplona, Spain.
